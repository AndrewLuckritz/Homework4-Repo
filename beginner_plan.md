## Overview

This page outlines a simple, beginner-friendly weekly workout plan that balances strength, cardio, and rest.

---

## Weekly Plan

### Monday – Full Body Strength

- Squats  
- Push-ups  
- Dumbbell rows  
- Planks  

### Tuesday – Light Cardio

- Brisk walk  
- Light jog  
- Cycling  

### Wednesday – Rest or Light Stretching

### Thursday – Strength Training (Upper Body)

- Shoulder press  
- Lat pulldown or pull-ups  
- Chest press  
- Bicep curls  

### Friday – Cardio Session

- Running  
- Rowing  
- Swimming  

### Saturday – Flexibility and Mobility

- Stretching routine  
- Yoga session  

### Sunday – Rest

---

## Tips for Beginners

- Start with manageable weight and intensity  
- Focus on proper form before increasing weight  
- Stay consistent rather than pushing too hard  
- Adjust based on how your body feels
