## Overview

This page covers several types of workout regimens. Each regimen has a different purpose depending on the fitness goal.

---

## Strength Training

Strength training focuses on building muscle and developing overall power.

Common methods include:

- Free weights
- Machines
- Bodyweight exercises

Benefits include:

- Increased muscle mass
- Enhanced metabolism
- Better functional strength

---

## Cardio Training

Cardiovascular training improves heart and lung health.

Examples include:

- Running
- Cycling
- Swimming
- Rowing

Benefits include:

- Improved endurance
- Better heart health
- Increased calorie burn

---

## High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)

HIIT involves short bursts of intense exercise followed by brief rest periods.

Typical structure:

1. 30 seconds of fast-paced exercise  
2. 15–30 seconds of rest  
3. Repeat for 10–20 minutes

Benefits include:

- Efficient calorie burning
- Time-effective workouts
- Improved conditioning

---

## Flexibility and Mobility Training

Flexibility training helps maintain range of motion in the muscles and joints.

Examples:

- Stretching
- Yoga
- Dynamic warmups

Benefits:

- Reduced injury risk
- Better posture
- Improved movement quality
