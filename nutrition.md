## Overview

This page provides foundational nutrition information to support healthy eating and fitness goals.

---

## Macronutrients

Macronutrients are nutrients the body needs in larger amounts. They provide energy and support essential functions.

### Protein

- Helps repair and build muscle
- Examples: chicken, eggs, beans, tofu

### Carbohydrates

- Main source of energy
- Examples: rice, pasta, fruit, oats

### Fats

- Supports hormones and organ function
- Examples: nuts, olive oil, avocado

---

## Hydration

Proper hydration is essential for:

- Physical performance  
- Digestion  
- Temperature regulation  

General recommendation: aim for consistent hydration throughout the day, adjusting based on activity level.

---

## Daily Eating Tips

- Include a source of protein with most meals  
- Prioritize whole foods over processed foods  
- Eat a balance of protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats  
- Avoid skipping meals, especially before workouts
